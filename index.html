🔐 Backup & Restore

Create a PIN-encrypted backup containing your name, debts, savings, income and expenses. Keep your backup and PIN safe. A 4-digit PIN is easy to guess and offers weak protection for financial backups; use 8–12 digits for better security. A lost PIN cannot be recovered.

On iPhone, save the downloaded file in Files → iCloud Drive. On Android, save to Files or Google Drive. Backups are not uploaded automatically unless Firebase sync is configured and enabled.
